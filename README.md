# Hung Jury

A courtroom bluffing game for adults. One player secretly knows the truth, one is fixing the jury, and everyone else has a few minutes to argue their way to a verdict. 37 cases, absurd and serious.

Play with 1 to 10 people. Computer jurors fill the empty seats: each has a personality, gets a secret role and exhibit, talks in the jury room in speech bubbles (and out loud, where the device can speak), bluffs, accuses and votes. With more than one person, the device is passed around for the secret parts.

Play it at https://chrisvonderau.github.io/hung-jury/ on an iPhone, iPad or Mac. The whole game is the single `index.html` file; names and played cases are remembered on the device only.
